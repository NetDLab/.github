## Networks, Dynamics, and Learning Laboratory

NetDLab is a research group in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at
Johns Hopkins University, led by [Enrique Mallada](https://mallada.ece.jhu.edu/). We
develop control, optimization, and learning theory for dynamical systems that must adapt
without losing their guarantees, and apply it to safe autonomy and to the power grids and
electricity markets of the energy transition.

### Software

- [**pyDDRV**](https://github.com/NetDLab/pyDDRV): data-driven verification of stability
  and regions of attraction from simulated trajectories, using Recurrent Lyapunov
  Functions.

More tools are on the way. See our [software page](https://mallada.ece.jhu.edu/software/).

### Teaching

- [**FoRL**](https://github.com/NetDLab/FoRL): course materials for Foundations of
  Reinforcement Learning at Johns Hopkins University.
- [**FoRL-EP**](https://github.com/NetDLab/FoRL-EP): lab assignments for Foundations of
  Reinforcement Learning, Johns Hopkins Engineering for Professionals.
